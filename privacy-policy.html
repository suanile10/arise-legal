Privacy Policy

Arise: Your Daily Booster
Effective date: 08 Aug. 2026
Last updated: 08 Aug. 2026

The short version: Arise itself does not collect any personal information about you. Everything you create in the app — your notification times, your habit tracker, your saved quotes — stays on your phone and is never sent to us. However, the app displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob, and Google collects certain device information in order to show those ads. You control this through the consent choices described below.

1. Who We Are

Arise: Your Daily Booster ("the app") is developed and published by SUNIL LAMA TAMANG, operating as Fit With Sunil. We are the data controller for the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact: fitwithsunilg@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect Ourselves

None. We operate no servers and no accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, health data, or any identifier. There is no sign-up, no login, and no way for you to send us personal information through the app.

3. Information Stored On Your Device

The app saves the following locally on your phone. This never leaves your device and we cannot see it:

  • Your chosen notification times and which reminders are switched on
  • Sound and vibration preferences
  • Quotes you have saved as favourites
  • Your 100-day habit tracker progress and start date

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling deletes it permanently and it cannot be recovered by you or by us.

4. Advertising

The app displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)Serving and measuring ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itRegional ad relevance, fraud prevention
Device type, operating system version, app versionAd compatibility and reporting
Ad interaction data (views, taps)Measurement and billing

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent party under its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We receive only aggregate, anonymous performance reports (such as total impressions and earnings). We never receive information that identifies you.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads, which prevents it being used to build an advertising profile.

5. Consent (EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form the first time you open it, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. This form asks whether you agree to personalised advertising.

Our legal basis for processing advertising data is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). If you decline, you will still see advertisements, but they will be non-personalised and will not use your Advertising ID to build a profile.

You may change or withdraw your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

6. Notifications

The app can send you motivational notifications at times you choose. On Android 13 and later your device will ask permission before any notification is sent, and you may withdraw that permission at any time in your device settings.

All notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device from quotes bundled inside the app. Nothing is delivered through our servers or any push service, and we receive no information about what you open or when.

7. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

8. Data Retention

Because we hold no personal data, we retain none. Data stored on your device is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app. Advertising data collected by Google is retained under Google's own retention schedule, set out in its privacy policy.

9. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, and similar laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, and port your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent.

Since we hold no personal data about you, we have nothing to retrieve, correct, export, or erase on request. To exercise rights over advertising data processed by Google, please use Google's own privacy tools, or contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will help you direct the request.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK and believe your rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

10. International Transfers

Google may process advertising data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards described in Google's privacy policy such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Data Security

We store no personal data, so there is no central database of ours that could be breached. Information saved on your device is protected by your device's operating system security and any screen lock you use.

12. Changes To This Policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy, revise the "Last updated" date, and where legally required ask for your consent inside the app before the change takes effect.
